Safe by design

  • Draft-only. A human approves anything public, money-related, a membership decision or an introduction.
  • Private by default. Emails are masked (a***@…), the member list is never returned, and contact details are never shared without consent.
  • Fake demo data. The demo runs on fake member data only.
  • AI disclosure and GDPR opt-in. Grodd says it is an AI assistant, and matching needs explicit opt-in consent.
