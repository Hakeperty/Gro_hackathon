# Grodd: hackathon kit (Gro to the Future, 2026-10-21)

**Brief:** "Build your own AI agent in just 45 minutes." Paid Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot. Solo or teams of up to 3.
**Prizes:** Best Business Value ("saves real money, time, or headaches") · Most Creative ("outside the box") · Greatest Human Impact ("makes lives genuinely better") · Most Unnecessary Build ("gloriously over-engineered").

## 1. Setup in 3 minutes (Claude.ai)
1. Go to claude.ai → **Projects** → **New project** and call it **"Grodd 🌱"**.
2. Paste everything inside the box in §2 into **Project instructions**.
3. Start a chat and run the demo prompts in §3. Rehearse once, so the judges see a clean run.

## 3. Demo script (3 minutes)

**Demo A: Best Business Value** ("headaches saved")
> *"Skapa Gro Breakfast varje onsdag 08:30–09:30 i Workshop Area, tema 'AI för bokföring' med Spiris, medlemmar gratis."* ("Create Gro Breakfast every Wednesday 08:30–09:30 in the Workshop Area, theme 'AI for bookkeeping' with Spiris, members free.")
→ One recurring series instead of 46 clones, plus Luma, LinkedIn (sv/en), Instagram and newsletter copy, with a consistency check.

Then:
> *"Audit our site: what should Robin fix first?"*
→ A prioritised fix list: the 7- vs 30-day trial conflict, the broken mailto link, the stale October 2025 text, the US terms template.

**Demo B: Greatest Human Impact**
> Paste a fake Pulse: *"Insight: first paying customer! Focus: pricing page. Blockers: no idea how to do VAT on SaaS, feel alone."*
→ Grodd gives a 2-line summary, a next step, a Gro resource (Spiris perk, Lunch & Learn), and "meet a member who has done SaaS VAT". Then:
> *"Write a kind nudge for a member who missed 2 pulses."*
→ Shows the "nobody falls through the cracks" angle.

**Demo C: Most Unnecessary Build**
> *"Fika Forecast for next Wednesday: 23 RSVPs, raining, week before payday."*
→ An over-engineered kanelbulle (cinnamon bun) order with a risk register.

## 4. 60-second pitch

> "Gro has 100+ members, three membership tiers, rooms, partners and events every week. All of it is run by basically one person across Circle, Luma, Swish, Brevo and Gmail.
> The site shows the cost: **46 breakfast posts cloned by hand, 37 cloned room bookings**, and two pages telling new members different free-trial lengths.
> Meet **Grodd 🌱**. One sentence in, a whole event out on every channel, in Swedish and English, checked for consistency. It audits the site and hands Robin a fix list.
> And here's the human part: Impact members already write a weekly Gro Pulse. Grodd reads every pulse, finds the blocker and suggests who you should meet. Nobody who asks for help stays stuck alone.
> It's draft-only, a human approves, and it's GDPR-minded. Next step: hook it into Circle's official API.
> Oh, and it orders the kanelbullar."

## 5. Likely jury questions
- **"Is it safe with member data?"** It only drafts. A human approves anything public, any money matter and any introduction. Matching is opt-in, and we'd use only the official Circle API or Circle MCP, because the site's terms ban unofficial bots and scrapers.
- **"What does it save?"** Every week: one event set up once instead of 4–5 times, zero cloned posts, and FAQ answers taken off the operator's desk.
- **"What's next?"** Circle API plus Luma webhooks, a member-ticket check (free member tickets on Luma aren't verified today), and a monthly board and funder report.
- Full plan: see `gro-community-agent-brief.md` §8–§9.

*Use only fake member data in the demo.*
